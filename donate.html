DONATION FAQS

How do I get my 80G Tax Exemption Certificate?

When you provide your PAN number during online donation or email your bank transfer transaction ID to info@blacksocialteamfoundation.org, our accounts team will issue an official Form 10BE tax certificate directly to your email.

What is your Donation Refund Policy?

In the rare case of accidental duplicate payment or clerical transaction errors, we process a full refund within 3 to 4 working days upon receiving your request at info@blacksocialteamfoundation.org.

Are my payment details secure?

Yes, all online payments are processed through RBI-compliant, 256-bit encrypted Razorpay and bank payment gateways. We never store or share your payment credentials.
