## 2. Core Mission & Vision Statements

### Mission Statement:
> *"We believe that every child deserves the chance to grow, learn, and thrive. Our mission is to support children in need by providing access to education, nutritious meals, clothing, and the resources they need to build a better future."*

### Key Organizational Goals:
1. **Old-Age Home:** Establishing a dedicated Old-Age Home for elderly individuals without support.
2. **Animal Shelter:** Creating a shelter home and rescue system for street animals.
3. **Schools for Underprivileged:** Building and supporting schools for underprivileged children.
4. **24/7 Ambulance Services:** Providing emergency medical transport and immediate response.
5. **Child Cancer & Handicapped Support:** Offering medical intervention and life-saving funding.
6. **Monthly Community Reach:** Reaching **500 to 1,000 homeless and needy people every month** with essential aid and meals.
7. **Women Empowerment:** Programs dedicated to uplifting and skilling women.

## 8. Activities, Campaigns & Programs

1. **Plant Distribution Drive**
   - *Partner/Location:* Joy Avenue
   - *Description:* Free baby plant distribution in collaboration with Joy Avenue.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Save Nature for Safe Future"* / *"Only a tree can give you free oxygen, which is very necessary."*
2. **Sports Shoes Distribution**
   - *Location:* Karna Badhir Vidyalaya (School for Deaf & Mute Children)
   - *Description:* Distribution of quality sports footwear to specially-abled students.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Champions Keep Playing"*
3. **Orphanage Meal Program**
   - *Description:* Sponsoring full-day nutritious meals for children at orphanage homes.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Think Before You Waste Your Food!"*
4. **Surplus Fruits Distribution Drive**
   - *Description:* Collecting surplus fresh fruits (portions for 50-100 people) and serving hungry families.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Love Fruits, Hate Waste"*
5. **Food Distribution & Slum Feeding Campaign**
   - *Description:* Preparing and serving balanced meals to improve nutrition for street and slum dwellers.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Food for Underprivileged Areas"*
6. **Tribal Family Grocery Kits**
   - *Description:* Essential monthly food rations and ration kits supplied to marginalized tribal families.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Grocery Support to the Tribal Families"*
7. **Volunteer Birthday Celebrations with Orphanage Kids**
   - *Description:* Organizing birthday events with underprivileged children to bring smiles and happiness.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Birthday Celebration with Underprivileged Children"*
8. **Special Education & Creative Handicraft Sessions**
   - *Target Group:* Deaf and Mute Students
   - *Description:* Learning creative craft techniques; students made chairs from recycled cardboard.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"We Never Forget That Beautiful Moment"*
9. **Cyber Bullying Awareness & Protest**
   - *Description:* Campaigning against digital harassment, trolling, and cyber abuse.
   - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Never Hurt Anyone with Your Negative Comments"*
10. **Street Animal Feeding & Dog Care**
    - *Description:* Daily feeding drives for stray dogs and promoting street dog adoption.
    - *Motto/Slogan:* *"Don't hit them, they are speechless" / "Adopt a street dog so they also get love"*
