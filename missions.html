OUR MISSION & VISION

Dedicated to making a tangible, grassroots difference through compassionate care, child healthcare, animal protection, and education.

"As an organization, our main mission is to open an Old-Age Home, an Animal Shelter, free Schools for Underprivileged Children, Women Empowerment centers, and direct support for Child Cancer and differently-abled patients. We reach 500 to 1000 homeless and needy people every month to help them lead dignified lives."

— Black Social Team Foundation Charter & Declaration
✦ Strategic Focus ✦

THE 7 CORE PILLARS

Our multi-dimensional framework for lasting social transformation.

1. Old-Age Sanctuary

Establishing residential sanctuary homes for elderly individuals who have been abandoned or lack family support, providing 24/7 medical oversight, nutritious meals, companionship, and emotional dignity.

2. Animal Shelter & Rescue

Conducting daily stray dog feeding rounds, rescuing injured and sick animals from streets, providing veterinary medical aid, and facilitating compassionate street animal adoption.

3. Slum Children Schools

Building accessible learning centers and subsidizing formal education, books, stationery kits, and school uniforms for children living in urban slums and underserved settlements.

4. 24/7 Emergency Ambulance

Setting up free and subsidized emergency medical transport services to carry critical slum patients, accident victims, and underprivileged citizens to tertiary hospitals without delays.

5. Child Cancer & Critical Care

Direct fundraising and hospital liaison for children diagnosed with Leukemia, Thalassemia Major, pediatric surgical deformities, and critical illnesses requiring immediate intervention.

6. Women Empowerment

Providing vocational training workshops in cosmetology, salon management, nail art, and tailoring so that women from marginalized backgrounds can earn independent livelihoods.

7. 500–1000 Monthly Rations

Conducting monthly dry grocery ration distribution drives to tribal and slum families, alongside prepared hot meals and surplus fruit drives to eradicate daily hunger.

✦ Forward Vision ✦

OUR 2026–2028 MILESTONES

Phase 1: Facility Land

Acquiring property on the outskirts of Mumbai for the upcoming BSTF Senior Living & Animal Shelter.

Phase 2: Mobile Clinic

Launching a customized Mobile Health Van providing weekly OPD checks and free medicines in slums.

Phase 3: 2000 Meals/Day

Expanding daily hot meal distribution kitchens across additional underserved municipal zones.
