TRANSPARENCY & POLICIES

Privacy Statement

Black Social Team Foundation is committed to ensuring the privacy of all our donors, beneficiaries, and website visitors. We have long campaigned for the right to privacy and against unauthorized surveillance. This policy represents our commitment as an organization to your privacy, giving you a transparent explanation about how your information is handled.

Refunds of Donations (3–4 Working Days)

Donations made to Black Social Team Foundation via our website can be refunded in exceptional circumstances (for example, duplicate payment deductions or clerical transaction errors). We will process the refund back to the source bank account within 3 to 4 working days upon written notice to info@blacksocialteamfoundation.org.

Information Security Standards

We take the security of personal data very seriously. Submissions travel via high-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. Donor records are stored in restricted-access directories accessible solely to trained, authorized accounts personnel.
